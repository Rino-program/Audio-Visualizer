Audio Visualizer is an Android app for playing local audio and video files while showing visual effects.

Features:
 - Local audio and video playback
 - Bulk import from the device music library (MediaStore)
 - Title, artist, and album art display
 - Background playback with notification, lock-screen, and headset controls
 - Restore the last track and playback position
 - Keep-screen-on option
 - 9 visualizer modes
 - Real-time microphone input visualization
 - 8-band equalizer and playback settings persistence
 - Video display options: audio only / window / background
 - Adjustable text and UI size from 80% to 130%
 - Offline design without Google services

Note: Video export and recording are not supported yet.
